## 1. Composition change

The parallel architecture is unchanged.

Changes are limited to how accepted evidence families are composed for new opportunity classes:

- CANDIDATE = opportunity awareness only; no Execution PREPARANDO;
- STRONG = may enter PREPARANDO;
- ACCEPTED = may enter ARMADO directly when RSI/HTF supports and momentum is not strongly opposing;
- source participation is remembered when the source bar qualified as STRONG;
- ACCEPTED confirmation may use current PSE CONFIRM or remembered source participation;
- fresh MTE acceleration is no longer re-required after HOLD_2 if momentum remains non-opposing.

MTE/RSE/PSE state definitions themselves are unchanged.

Frozen Execution 0.1 remains independent and unmodified.
