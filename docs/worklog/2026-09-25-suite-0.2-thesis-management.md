## 8. Exact next discriminant

Without changing accepted MTE/RSE/PSE definitions, compare preregistered candidate channels:

### PROTECT_STRICT
Candidate evidence:
- invalidation within accepted 0.20 ATR; OR
- Strength EXHAUSTED; OR
- structural warning **plus at least one deterioration family**.

Purpose:
- remove bare structure-only PROTECT noise while retaining adverse-thesis warnings.

### FAVORABLE_FADING
Diagnostic realization candidate:
- thesis has made positive close progress from confirmation toward target; AND
- Strength is FADING or worse;
- not already terminal.

### FAVORABLE_EXHAUSTED
More selective realization candidate:
- positive close progress; AND
- Strength is EXHAUSTED / REACTION_RISK;
- not terminal.

Compare:
- live-bar saturation;
- state/churn load;
- completed vs invalidated episode reach;
- lead time to COMPLETED / INVALIDATED;
- progress at first warning;
- source-path behavior.

Do not choose a realization rule by maximizing hit rate.
The objective is semantic separation:
- adverse deterioration -> PROTECT;
- favorable mature deterioration -> REALIZATION_RISK.

No production Pine/default/profile/panel change.
