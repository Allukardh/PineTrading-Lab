## 12. Preregistered Management V1

The V1 rule is fixed before implementation/cross-asset testing.

### Terminal precedence

1. AMBIGUOUS
2. COMPLETED
3. INVALIDATED

Same truth semantics as V0.

### PROTECT

If non-terminal:
- invalidation within accepted 0.20 ATR; OR
- Strength EXHAUSTED;

**unless** the thesis is already in the favorable realization zone described below.

Bare structural conflict / map-direction loss remains diagnostic only.

### REALIZATION_RISK

If non-terminal and invalidation is not near:

1. existing accepted destination-near REACTION_RISK remains valid; OR
2. frozen confirmation-to-target path progress >= **0.50** and Strength is FADING or worse.

Rationale:
- 0.50 is the semantic midpoint of the frozen structural path;
- no outcome-driven threshold search;
- deterioration before midpoint remains protection/diagnostic context;
- deterioration after midpoint describes thesis maturity / realization attention.

### CONTINUATION

No terminal, PROTECT or REALIZATION_RISK condition.

### Precedence between non-terminal warnings

1. invalidation-near -> PROTECT
2. REALIZATION_RISK
3. EXHAUSTED -> PROTECT
4. CONTINUATION

This prevents favorable mature exhaustion from being mislabeled as adverse protection.

### Structural warning

Keep in diagnostics only for V1.

Do not surface structure-only PROTECT.

## 24. Anchor architecture decision

### Synthetic fallback target/invalidation
**DO NOT CREATE**

No evidence supports inventing geometry merely to reach 100% full-anchor coverage.

### Binary anchored / unsupported management
**REFINE**

Management capabilities are independent.

Preregister four explicit anchor classes:

- `FULL` — target + invalidation
- `INVALIDATION_ONLY` — invalidation present, target absent
- `TARGET_ONLY` — target present, invalidation absent
- `NONE` — neither present

Management semantics under partial anchors:

- Strength-based CONTINUATION / PROTECT remains available for every confirmed directional thesis.
- invalidation-near and INVALIDATED require an invalidation anchor.
- midpoint/destination REALIZATION_RISK and COMPLETED require a target anchor.
- missing capability remains explicit; no synthetic level is substituted.
- outcome denominators must be capability-aware.
