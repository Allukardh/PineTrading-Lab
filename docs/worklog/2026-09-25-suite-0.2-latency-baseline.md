## Research measurement corrections made before interpretation

The initial harness exposed three measurement problems that were corrected **without changing 0.1**:

1. same-direction Market Map thesis replacement originally truncated an opportunity episode even though Execution 0.1 does not use thesis identity as a reset boundary;
2. an already-`ALINHADO` Execution state at independent opportunity confirmation was initially treated as absence of a new confirmation instead of correct anticipation;
3. regime-transition outcome originally had no practical time horizon, allowing a candidate to be called "matured" months later.

The final report therefore:
- keeps same-direction thesis churn diagnostic-only;
- treats already-`ALINHADO` as anticipated/covered;
- reports regime transition maturation in 12 / 24 / 48-bar observation windows.

## Independent opportunity labels

Current first-pass labels are intentionally independent of Execution result:

- `BREAKOUT_CANDIDATE` — coherent structural break at the break close;
- `PULLBACK_RETEST` — first accepted reaction/touch/retest/reclaim per thesis;
- `REGIME_TRANSITION_CANDIDATE` — first opposite structural break against a mature prior confirmed regime;
- `REGIME_REVERSAL` — new opposite regime only when the accepted Market Map is itself coherent in that direction.

`BREAKOUT_CANDIDATE` deliberately does **not** claim every structural break is a tradable expansion.

## Breakout discrimination

Retrospective outcome is diagnostic only and never available on the breakout bar.

### 4H

- held after accepted 6-bar fakeout window: **543**
- fakeout: **580**

- held: **22.28%** confirmed or already aligned;

Interpretation:
- 0.1 selectivity already rejects a meaningful share of fakeouts;
- simply making every structural break actionable would discard useful selectivity;
- nevertheless ~78% of held 4H breakouts are not covered by a confirmation/aligned state, and `LOCATION_NOT_RELEVANT` dominates the misses.

Coverage is low on both held and fakeout daily breaks (4.26% vs 6.54%), so generic 0.1 Execution is not a useful breakout discriminator on 1D.

## Early regime-transition diagnosis

A first opposite structural break against a mature regime is **too broad** to become an actionable 0.2 opportunity by itself.

### 4H — 607 candidates

- matured into a coherent opposite `REGIME_REVERSAL` within 12 bars: **11**
- within 24 bars: **19**
- within 48 bars: **35**
- unresolved within those respective windows: 596 / 588 / 572

### 1D — 95 candidates

- matured within 12 bars: **1**
- within 24 bars: **2**
- within 48 bars: **4**

The unrestricted retrospective "matured" median was ~802.5 bars on 4H, proving that an unlimited-horizon maturity label is operationally meaningless.

Decision:
- **do not** weaken canonical `map_dir`;
- **do not** promote a raw opposite break as a new actionable direction;
- research an intermediate transition/acceptance contract before any `candidate_dir` can become operator-facing.

## First product conclusions

### 1. 0.1 is not globally "too slow"

For pullback/retest:
- 4H is already PREP+ on the event in ~55% of episodes;
- 1D is already PREP+ in ~63%.

This supports keeping the accepted pullback/retest behavior as the reference specialty.

### 2. Breakout coverage is structurally narrow

The dominant blocker is not usually "RSI waited too long"; it is that 0.1 only regards correction/retest/reclaim locations as Execution-relevant.

This is direct evidence for an Opportunity Engine rather than blind threshold relaxation.

### 3. Early regime transition needs a separate semantic

The canonical Market Map being neutral/conflicted during an early opposite break is honest and useful.

A future 0.2 design may carry a separate provisional transition direction, but only after acceptance/follow-through evidence is demonstrated. The safe `map_dir` must not be weakened merely to create earlier signals.

### 4. Profiles are still premature

This evidence justifies broader opportunity-location research.

It does **not yet** justify shipping ANTECIPADO / CONFIRMADO profiles. First determine which candidate opportunity semantics deserve to exist.

## Next discriminant
