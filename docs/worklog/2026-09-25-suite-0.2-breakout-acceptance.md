## 1. Breakout feature evidence

Across BTC / ETH / AVAX, the most repeatable contemporaneous breakout discriminants are:

1. **structural break penetration normalized by ATR**;
2. directional candle body/range;
3. **relative volume / accepted PSE confirmation**;
4. RSI directional step;
5. MTE acceleration as a weaker secondary correlate.

The strongest and most stable single feature across the three assets is break penetration in ATR.

Examples of held-vs-fakeout oriented AUC for break penetration:
- BTC 4H: ~0.706
- BTC 1D: ~0.694
- ETH 4H: ~0.690
- ETH 1D: ~0.736
- AVAX 4H: ~0.720
- AVAX 1D: ~0.659

Relative volume is also consistently higher in held breakouts, but with lower standalone separation.

### Decision — feature families

- **break penetration / structural acceptance — KEEP**
- **PSE participation confirmation — KEEP**
- **candle body/range — KEEP as diagnostics / possible follow-through evidence**
- **RSI directional step — KEEP as diagnostics, not a breakout gate yet**
- **MTE candidate-bar requirement — REMOVE from the minimal breakout-acceptance gate**

Important:
removing MTE from the **breakout acceptance gate** does **not** remove MTE from Execution timing. Evidence only says it adds little incremental discrimination at the structural-break acceptance boundary.

## 2. Immediate acceptance is too permissive as final confirmation

A fixed research criterion of:
- break penetration >= 0.50 ATR;
- accepted PSE `CONFIRM`;

improves held-share among accepted breakouts, but still accepts enough fakeouts that it should not become final breakout confirmation on the candidate bar.

Cross-asset examples:

### 4H — PENETRATION_PSE

- BTC: held recall ~49%, fakeout accepted ~22%, held share ~68%
- ETH: held recall ~46%, fakeout accepted ~20%, held share ~67%
- AVAX: held recall ~43%, fakeout accepted ~12%, held share ~78%

### 1D — PENETRATION_PSE

- BTC: held recall ~44%, fakeout accepted ~15%, held share ~72%
- ETH: held recall ~54%, fakeout accepted ~19%, held share ~73%
- AVAX: held recall ~32%, fakeout accepted ~15%, held share ~63%

Decision:
- **do not** equate a strong breakout candle with final confirmation;
- use strong candidate-bar evidence as an **early/provisional quality state** only.

## 3. Follow-through is the robust confirmation anchor

Requiring two subsequent confirmed closes to remain beyond the broken structural level (`HOLD_2`) is the strongest simple cross-asset confirmation anchor tested so far.

### 4H — HOLD_2

- BTC: held recall **97.24%**, fakeout accepted **34.48%**, held share **72.53%**, median displacement **0.24 ATR**
- ETH: held recall **97.09%**, fakeout accepted **38.71%**, held share **68.68%**, median displacement **0.33 ATR**
- AVAX: held recall **95.19%**, fakeout accepted **30.75%**, held share **74.95%**, median displacement **0.34 ATR**

### 1D — HOLD_2

- BTC: held recall **100%**, fakeout accepted **33.64%**, held share **72.31%**, median displacement **0.35 ATR**
- ETH: held recall **100%**, fakeout accepted **33.67%**, held share **74.42%**, median displacement **0.37 ATR**
- AVAX: held recall **98.31%**, fakeout accepted **43.66%**, held share **65.17%**, median displacement **0.20 ATR**

Decision:
- **HOLD_2 — KEEP as the confirmed breakout-acceptance anchor**;
- do not interpret held-share as win rate or trade expectancy.

## 4. Staged early acceptance

Research also tested:

`PENETRATION + PSE + HOLD_1; otherwise HOLD_2`

This preserves almost all HOLD_2 held coverage while allowing the strongest subset to advance one bar earlier.

Examples:

### BTC
- 4H: recall 97.24%, fakeout 39.66%, held share 69.66%, median displacement 0.12 ATR
- 1D: recall 100%, fakeout 35.51%, held share 71.21%, median displacement 0.20 ATR

### ETH
- 4H: recall 97.09%, fakeout 40.92%, held share 67.48%, median displacement 0.23 ATR
- 1D: recall 100%, fakeout 35.71%, held share 73.28%, median displacement 0.23 ATR

### AVAX
- 4H: recall 95.45%, fakeout 33.85%, held share 73.16%, median displacement 0.25 ATR
- 1D: recall 98.31%, fakeout 49.30%, held share 62.37%, median displacement 0.23 ATR

Interpretation:
- gaining one bar earlier has a measurable fakeout cost;
- the cost is modest on BTC/ETH but more visible on AVAX 1D;
- therefore the +1 state should be **provisional/arming evidence**, not the same semantic as +2 confirmation.

Candidate Opportunity/Execution mapping for later counterfactual work:

```text
structural break close
    -> BREAKOUT_CANDIDATE

decisive penetration + PSE + first held close
    -> BREAKOUT_STRONG / candidate for PREPARANDO or ARMADO

two confirmed closes holding structure
    -> BREAKOUT_ACCEPTED / candidate for CONFIRMA
```

This mapping is not yet production-authorized.

## 5. MTE does not belong in the minimal breakout-acceptance gate

Adding MTE alignment to the candidate-bar or staged acceptance rule generally changes quality only marginally while reducing or delaying coverage.

Decision:
- **REMOVE MTE as a mandatory structural-break acceptance gate**;
- retain MTE in downstream Execution timing/strength where it already has an accepted independent role.

This reduces semantic duplication.

## 6. Regime-transition candidate remains non-actionable

The first opposite structural break against a mature regime remains far too broad to become an entry signal.

Fast maturation into a strict coherent opposite regime is rare.

### 4H — matured within 48 bars

- BTC: 35 positive vs ~570 negative
- ETH: 23 positive vs ~621 negative
- AVAX: 21 positive vs ~424 negative

### 1D — matured within 48 bars

- BTC: 4 positive vs ~91 negative
- ETH: 4 positive vs ~102 negative
- AVAX: 5 positive vs ~48 negative

There are recurring hints:
- stronger directional MTE core;
- stronger RSI center alignment;
- more decisive break penetration in several slices;
- shorter prior-regime duration appears associated in multiple 4H slices.

However:
- separation is not stable enough across assets/features to define an actionable rule;
- the 1D positive sample remains very small;
- using shorter prior-regime duration as a gate would be especially questionable for the desired **major bear-to-bull cycle reversal** use case.

Decision:
- `REGIME_TRANSITION_CANDIDATE` — **KEEP as provisional research/diagnostic state**
- raw transition candidate as trade opportunity — **REMOVE**
- strict `REGIME_REVERSAL` — **KEEP**
- early actionable regime-reversal rule — **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**

Next research should focus on structural **base/acceptance/follow-through** rather than weakening canonical `map_dir`.

## 7. Product consequence

Suite 0.2 should not solve responsiveness by globally relaxing Execution thresholds.

The evidence supports a new **Opportunity lifecycle**:

```text
CANDIDATE
  -> STRONG / SUSTAINED
  -> ACCEPTED
  -> Execution timing
```

Different opportunity families may move through those stages differently.

For breakout:
- candidate = structural break close;
- strong/sustained = decisive penetration + participation + first held close;
- accepted = two held closes beyond structure.

This is compatible with the existing `PREPARANDO -> ARMADO -> CONFIRMA` vocabulary and can increase internal intelligence without adding panel clutter.
