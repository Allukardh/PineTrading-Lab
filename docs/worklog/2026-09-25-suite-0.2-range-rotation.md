## 1. Contract tested

A range requires:
- two confirmed swing-high cycles;
- two confirmed swing-low cycles;
- all highs structurally above all lows;
- boundary drift <= 20% of range height;
- range height >= 2 ATR;
- new compatible pivots may update the same range identity.

A rotation candidate requires:
- interaction with one structural edge;
- confirmed close back inside the box;
- same-edge opportunities do not repeat until price traverses back through midpoint.

Two trigger families were measured:
- `EDGE_REJECTION`;
- `SWEEP_RECLAIM` from already-calculated MM-0 raw reclaim telemetry near the edge.

Outcome is retrospective structural behavior:
- OPPOSITE_REACHED;
- MID_REACHED;
- FAILED_BEFORE_MID;
- CENSORED.
