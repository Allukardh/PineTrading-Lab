- two confirmed swing-high cycles;
- two confirmed swing-low cycles;
- all highs structurally above all lows;
- boundary drift <= 20% of range height;
- range height >= 2 ATR;
- new compatible pivots may update the same range identity.

A rotation candidate requires:
- interaction with one structural edge;
- confirmed close back inside the box;
- same-edge opportunities do not repeat until price traverses back through midpoint.

## 21. Superseding preregistration — source-arm / acceptance-confirm

The unimplemented readiness-v2 preregistration in section 17 is superseded by
this evidence correction.

Two variants are now preregistered:

### EARLY_ANY1

On EDGE_REJECTION source close:
- if MTE strongly opposing **and** local RSI opposing: remain WAIT;
- otherwise, if at least one ignition family is present:
  - MTE aligned, or
  - local RSI supportive, or
  - PSE CONFIRM;
  -> enter ARMADO immediately;
- otherwise remain WAIT / opportunity awareness only.

On next-bar structural ACCEPTED:
- if the setup was already ARMADO from the same range source, chart-close
  acceptance emits CONFIRMA;
- no fresh MTE/RSE/PSE coincidence is required because structural persistence
  is the final confirmation evidence.

### SELECTIVE_ANY2

On EDGE_REJECTION source close:
- dual MTE+local-RSI opposition -> WAIT;
- one ignition family -> PREPARANDO;
- two or more ignition families -> ARMADO;
- zero -> WAIT.

On next-bar structural ACCEPTED:
- only an already-ARMADO setup emits CONFIRMA;
- PREPARANDO may remain contextual but does not become a late entry merely
  because midpoint may already have been traversed.

### Both variants

- HTF RSI is context, not veto;
- structural ACCEPTED remains mandatory for CONFIRMA;
- frame invalidation/opposite-edge completion cancels the setup;
- no indicator state definition changes;
- breakout/reacceleration Opportunity v2 unchanged;
- no hard regime filter;
- no production Pine/default/profile changes.
